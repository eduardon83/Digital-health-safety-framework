HDSF v0.3.0 guide

Glossary

One term per thing. HDSF texts use these words with these meanings (STYLE.md).

TermMeaning
Art. 9 conditionOne of the exceptions in GDPR Art. 9(2) that allow processing health data, for example explicit consent or health care under professional secrecy
BLOCK, REVIEW, WARN, HUMAN, WAIVEDThe five kinds of finding in a report: fix before launch; needs a decision; recommended fix; a person must check; accepted with a reason
Controller, processorGDPR roles: who decides why and how data is processed, and who processes it for them
CSEFChild-Safe EdTech Framework, the sister framework for children's digital products; it shares HDSF's engine
DPIAData protection impact assessment (GDPR Art. 35); in Portuguese AIPD
EHDSEuropean Health Data Space, Regulation (EU) 2025/327
EHR systemElectronic health record system, as defined in the EHDS
Engine (sfe)The shared code that validates rules, scans products and writes reports for CSEF and HDSF
FeatureSomething the product does that changes which rules apply (wearables, AI, chat, research use)
FindingA sign, found in the code, the manifest or the documents, that a rule may not be met
GDPR (RGPD)General Data Protection Regulation, Regulation (EU) 2016/679; in Portuguese Regulamento Geral sobre a Proteção de Dados
Health dataData concerning health in the sense of GDPR Art. 4(15), including data that reveals health when linked to a person
Intended purposeWhat the maker says the product is for, for whom and what it claims; it decides whether the product is a medical device
LevelHow binding a rule is: 1 law, 2 EU recommendations and law that applies later, 3 best practice from research
MakerThe company or person that makes the product (manufacturer, in MDR terms)
Manifesthdsf.yaml: one page that describes the product for HDSF
MDAIMedical device artificial intelligence: AI that is, or is part of, a medical device (MDCG 2019-11 rev. 1)
MDSWMedical device software
No-goA rule a company pack marks as never broken; it blocks at every level
PackA file that adds sources and rules to HDSF or makes rules stricter: a country pack (Portugal), a sector pack or a company pack (ACME)
ProfileA group of rules for a kind of product: core, W wellness, M medical device software, E EHR systems and EHDS, R research, T telehealth
QualificationThe decision whether a product is a medical device (MDR or IVDR), and which class
UsersThe people a product is for: the public, patients, professionals, children
WaiverA recorded, dated decision to accept a finding for now, with a reason
WorkbenchThe browser page where people without code describe a product, add documents and get reports
