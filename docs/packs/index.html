HDSF v0.3.0 guide

Packs: countries, sectors and companies

HDSF covers EU law. Packs add the rest: national law, a health system's rules, research you want cited or your own company's red lines. A pack can add sources and rules and make rules stricter. It can never weaken or remove a rule.

The two packs that ship with HDSF

PackFileHow it loadsWhat it does
Portugalpacks/pt.yaml (rules)By itself when hdsf.yaml lists PT in countriesConsent age 13; CNPD, INFARMED, CEIC and CNCS; 112 and SNS 24 in the urgent help route; information in Portuguese as a level 1 rule; texts in European Portuguese
ACME (example)examples/company-packs/acme.yamlListed under packs: in hdsf.yaml, or --packFictional company: minimum level 2; four no-go rules; forbidden packages and wording; decision records for AI and research; a review every 180 days; two ACME rules

Start a new product in Portugal from templates/hdsf-portugal.yaml. In the Workbench, use "Start from the Portugal template" and "Add the ACME company pack (example)".

What a pack can contain

SectionUse it for
parametersValues shown in reports: consent age, authorities, helplines, language
sourcesLaws, guidance, standards and research to cite
rulesNew rules; ids start with the pack id in capitals (PT-LNG-01)
amendMake an HDSF rule stricter: add_sources, note, level (SHOULD to MUST only), tier (a lower number only), add_patterns
policyCompany decisions: minimum_level, no_go, forbid_dependencies, forbid_phrases, forbid_patterns, require_decision_for_features, review_every_days

A policy section creates rules named <ID>-POL-01, -02 and so on, and a source <ID>-POLICY, so reports show which findings come from your policy and which from law.

Write your own

  1. Copy packs/_template.yaml.
  2. Give each source a type, a dated status, a link and a verified date (or pending).
  3. Check it: python3 tooling/build.py --validate-pack path/to/pack.yaml.
  4. Country packs go in packs/<code>.yaml in this repository; open a pull request with a note on who reviewed it. Company packs stay in your own repository, for example .hdsf/packs/acme.yaml.

Packs use the same engine as CSEF packs, so a company with products for children and health can keep the same policy shape in both.
