Law in force

GDPR

Regulation (EU) 2016/679, General Data Protection Regulation

Status. In force since 25 May 2018. Data concerning health, genetic and biometric data are special categories (Art. 9): processing is banned unless an Art. 9(2) condition applies, for example explicit consent (a), health or social care (h), public health (i) or research (j). DPIA (Art. 35), data protection by design (Art. 25), security (Art. 32), child consent for information society services (Art. 8)

Jurisdiction. EU · Verified. 2026-11-22

Rules citing it. CJEU-LINDEN

CJEU, C-21/23, Lindenapotheke, 4 Oct 2024

Status. Case law (Grand Chamber). Data customers enter when ordering pharmacy-only medicines online are data concerning health under Art. 9(1) GDPR, even without a prescription, where a link can be made between the product, its use and an identifiable person; certainty is not needed. Competitors can sue under unfair competition law

Jurisdiction. EU · Verified. 2026-11-22

Rules citing it. CORE-DAT-01, CORE-TRK-02

Link. https://curia.europa.eu/jcms/upload/docs/application/pdf/2024-10/cp240159en.pdf

DSA

Regulation (EU) 2022/2065, Digital Services Act

Status. Fully applicable since 17 Feb 2024. Online platforms may not show ads based on profiling with special categories of data such as health (Art. 26(3)) and may not use dark patterns (Art. 25)

Jurisdiction. EU · Verified. 2026-11-11

Rules citing it. CORE-COM-02, CORE-TRK-03

Link. https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2022/2065/oj

CJEU-SRB

CJEU, C-413/23 P, EDPS v Single Resolution Board, 4 Sep 2025 (EU:C:2025:645)

Status. Case law. Pseudonymised data stay personal for the controller that holds the key; for a recipient with no means reasonably likely to re-identify, they may not be personal data. The controller must name such recipients when it collects the data

Jurisdiction. EU · Verified. 2026-11-19

Rules citing it. CORE-DAT-11, R-DAT-02

MDR

Regulation (EU) 2017/745 on medical devices (MDR)

Status. Applies since 26 May 2021. Software with a medical intended purpose is a medical device (Art. 2(1)); Annex VIII Rule 11 classifies software. Clinical evaluation (Art. 61), quality management and post-market surveillance (Art. 10, 83 to 86), vigilance (Art. 87). Transitional periods for legacy devices were extended by Regulation (EU) 2023/607

Jurisdiction. EU · Verified. pending

Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, Artificial Intelligence Act

Status. Prohibitions (Art. 5) and AI literacy (Art. 4) apply since 2 Feb 2025. GPAI rules since 2 Aug 2025. As amended by the AI Omnibus: high-risk rules for Annex III use cases from 2 Dec 2027, and for AI in products under Annex I law (medical devices and IVDs included) from 2 Aug 2028

EHDS

Regulation (EU) 2025/327 on the European Health Data Space

Status. In force 26 Mar 2025; applies in stages: general date 26 Mar 2027, further milestones 26 Mar 2029, 2031 and 2035 (check the date for each chapter). Primary use: people's access to and control over their electronic health data; exchange of patient summaries and ePrescriptions in all Member States from 2029. EHR systems must meet Annex II (interoperability and logging components). A wellness application that claims interoperability with EHR systems must meet the same requirements, inform users, carry a label (Art. 47) and be registered in the EU database (Art. 49); interoperability never means automatic sharing (Art. 48). Secondary use covers data from wellness applications too

Jurisdiction. EU · Verified. 2026-11-22

Notes. Commission page and timeline: https://health.ec.europa.eu/ehealth-digital-health-and-care/european-health-data-space-regulation-ehds_en

Rules citing it. CORE-DAT-09, CORE-GOV-02, CORE-SEC-07, E-ACC-01, E-INT-01, E-INT-02, E-LOG-01, R-DAT-03, R-EHD-01, W-EHD-01, W-EHD-02

Link. https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/327/oj
