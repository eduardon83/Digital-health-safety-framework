Health Data Safety Framework

Open, checkable rules for digital health products in the EU: wellness apps, medical device software, health records, research platforms and telehealth. One place to see which law applies, what it asks for, and how to check your product.

94 rules, 61 sources. Each rule says what to do, why (with its source), how to improve and how it is checked: in the code, in a short manifest (hdsf.yaml), or by a person.

HDSF is a draft and not legal or regulatory advice. Whether software is a medical device is the manufacturer's decision under the MDR or IVDR; ask a regulatory expert or a notified body when in doubt.
