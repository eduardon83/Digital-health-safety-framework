E-ACC-01: People can restrict who sees parts of their record

MUST · Level 2 (Forward-compliant) · Users: all · Detection: manual · Effort: high

Requirement. The system lets people restrict access by professionals to all or part of their data, with an emergency override that is logged.

Why. EHDS rights of natural persons over their electronic health data (primary use).

How to improve. Add per-category restrictions and a logged break-glass route.

Sources. EHDS

E-DAT-01: Professionals see records only in a care relationship

MUST · Level 1 (Legal baseline) · Users: all · Detection: manual · Effort: medium

Requirement. Professionals can open a person's record only when they are part of that person's care, under professional secrecy. They see only the parts they need.

Why. GDPR Art. 9(2)(h) and 9(3).

How to improve. Tie access rights to care episodes; review access rights monthly.

National note. Portugal: only health professionals may access the clinical record (Lei 12/2005, Art. 5); access on a need-to-know basis (Lei 58/2019, Art. 29).

Sources. GDPR, PT-LEI-12-2005, PT-LEI-58-2019

E-INT-01: The EHR system meets the EHDS essential requirements

MUST · Level 2 (Forward-compliant) · Users: all · Detection: manifest · Effort: high

Requirement. An EHR system has the European interoperability component and the European logging component (EHDS Annex II), passes the digital testing environment, and carries the EU declaration of conformity and CE marking before it is placed on the market under the EHDS dates.

Why. EHDS Chapter III; the Commission describes European digital testing environments for manufacturers.

How to improve. Plan the conformity work against the EHDS dates; link the evidence in hdsf.yaml (ehds.conformity).

Sources. EHDS

E-INT-02: Data leaves in the European exchange format, without lock-in

MUST · Level 2 (Forward-compliant) · Users: all · Detection: manual · Effort: high

Requirement. The system exports priority categories (patient summaries, ePrescriptions, images, lab results, discharge reports) in the European electronic health record exchange format, and customers can switch provider without losing data or paying exit fees.

Why. EHDS (exchange format); Data Act Chapter VI (switching between data processing services).

How to improve. Implement the exchange format (FHIR based where specified); document the exit plan for customers.

Sources. EHDS, DATA-ACT, HL7-FHIR

E-INT-03: The EHR system follows the EU exchange format recommendation now

SHOULD · Level 2 (Forward-compliant) · Users: all · Detection: manual · Effort: high

Requirement. Until EHDS implementing acts apply, the system follows the principles and baseline specifications of Commission Recommendation (EU) 2019/243 for patient summaries, ePrescriptions, lab results, images and discharge reports.

Why. The recommendation is the basis for the European EHR exchange format that the EHDS makes binding.

How to improve. Map your data model to the recommended specifications; plan the gaps.

Sources. EC-REC-EHRXF, HL7-FHIR

E-LOG-01: People can see who accessed their record

MUST · Level 2 (Forward-compliant) · Users: all · Detection: manual · Effort: medium

Requirement. Every access to a person's electronic health data is logged, and the person can see who accessed it and when.

Why. EHDS (logging component and the right to information on access); GDPR Art. 15.

How to improve. Expose the access log in the patient portal; alert on unusual access.

Sources. EHDS, GDPR

R-EHD-01: The maker is ready to be an EHDS data holder

SHOULD · Level 2 (Forward-compliant) · Users: all · Detection: manual · Effort: medium

Requirement. The maker knows whether it will be a health data holder under the EHDS, can describe its datasets and has a plan for answering health data access bodies, including for wellness data.

Why. EHDS secondary use rules (from 2029 and 2031) cover data from wellness applications too; the EDPB and EDPS asked for limits on that.

How to improve. Write a short dataset description and a contact process for data access bodies.

Sources. EHDS, EDPB-EDPS-EHDS
