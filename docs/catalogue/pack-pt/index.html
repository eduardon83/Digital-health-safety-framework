PT-LNG-01: Health information is written in European Portuguese

SHOULD · Level 2 (Forward-compliant) · Users: all · Detection: mixed · Effort: low

Requirement. Health information, safety messages and privacy texts for people in Portugal use European Portuguese (pt-PT), not Brazilian Portuguese: "equipa", "registo", "contacto", "telemóvel", and the European forms of address and verbs.

Why. People understand health information best in their own variety of the language. Plain language guidance from the Commission and the Publications Office is available in European Portuguese.

How to improve. Review texts with a pt-PT speaker; keep a glossary of pt-PT terms; do not ship pt-BR machine translation.

Sources. EC-CLEAR, OP-PLAIN, PT-DL238-86

PT-SAF-01: The urgent help route names 112 and SNS 24

MUST · Level 2 (Forward-compliant) · Users: all · Features: symptom_input, mental_health, ai_generative, emergency_content, chat · Detection: manual · Effort: low

Requirement. For people in Portugal, the urgent help route shows 112 for emergencies and SNS 24 (808 24 24 24) for health advice, and works with one tap.

Why. These are the national numbers people know; CORE-SAF-01 asks for a route to urgent help.

How to improve. Add both numbers to the help screen and to AI and chat safety answers.

Sources. PLD, PT-SNS24

Sources added by the pack

  • PT-SNS24: SNS 24, Centro de Contacto do Serviço Nacional de Saúde. National health line, 808 24 24 24, every day, all hours: triage, advice and referral. Also online and in the SNS 24 app Verified: pending. Link
  • PT-INFARMED: INFARMED, I.P., competent authority for medical devices in Portugal. Receives vigilance reports (serious incidents, field safety corrective actions) and notifications of clinical investigations of medical devices Verified: pending. Link
  • PT-CEIC: CEIC, Comissão de Ética para a Investigação Clínica. National ethics committee for clinical investigation, including clinical investigations of medical devices Verified: pending. Link
  • PT-DL238-86: Decreto-Lei n.º 238/86, de 19 de agosto (uso da língua portuguesa na informação sobre bens e serviços). In force. Information about goods and services offered in Portugal must be given in Portuguese Verified: pending. Link
