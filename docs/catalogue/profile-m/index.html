M-AI-01: Medical AI is ready for the AI Act high-risk rules

MUST · Level 2 (Forward-compliant) · Users: all · Features: ai_clinical · Detection: manifest · Effort: high

Requirement. Medical device AI that needs a notified body meets the AI Act high-risk requirements (risk management, data governance, logging, transparency, human oversight, accuracy and robustness) by 2 Aug 2028, inside one technical file with the MDR documentation.

Why. Such AI is high-risk under AI Act Art. 6(1); the AI Act does not change the MDR class (MDCG 2025-6). The AI Omnibus sets 2 Aug 2028 for Annex I products.

How to improve. Add the AI Act sections to the technical file now; link the AI risk file in hdsf.yaml (ai.risk_file).

Sources. AIA, AIA-OMNIBUS, MDCG-2025-6

M-AI-02: Changes to models are controlled

MUST · Level 1 (Legal baseline) · Users: all · Features: ai_clinical · Detection: manual · Effort: medium

Requirement. Retraining, new models and new data sources go through change control with an assessment of whether the change is significant, and with verification on a locked test set before release.

Why. MDR Annex IX (changes to approved devices need notified body review when they are substantial); MDCG 2025-6 on AI changes.

How to improve. Version models and datasets; write the change assessment template; never auto-deploy a retrained model.

Sources. MDR, MDCG-2025-6

M-APP-01: The store listing shows device information before download

MUST · Level 1 (Legal baseline) · Users: all · Detection: manual · Effort: low

Requirement. In app stores and on the website, before download, people see the device name, manufacturer, intended purpose, CE marking, warnings and the electronic instructions for use. The app is listed as a medical device, apart from wellness apps.

Why. Uploading a device app to a platform is placing it on the market (MDCG 2025-4); MDR Art. 7 and Annex I section 23.

How to improve. Update store listings and the in-app "about" screen with the device information.

Sources. MDR, MDCG-2025-4

M-CLN-01: Clinical evaluation follows MDCG 2020-1

MUST · Level 1 (Legal baseline) · Users: all · Detection: manifest · Effort: high

Requirement. A clinical (or performance) evaluation shows valid clinical association, technical performance and clinical performance for the intended purpose, and is updated with post-market data.

Why. MDR Art. 61 and Annex XIV; IVDR Art. 56; MDCG 2020-1 for software.

How to improve. Write the clinical evaluation plan and report; link them in hdsf.yaml (clinical).

Sources. MDR, IVDR, MDCG-2020-1

M-PMS-01: Post-market surveillance and vigilance are running

MUST · Level 1 (Legal baseline) · Users: all · Detection: manual · Effort: medium

Requirement. A post-market surveillance plan collects complaints, incidents, app reviews and performance data; serious incidents are reported to the competent authority on time; trends are analysed.

Why. MDR Art. 83 to 92.

How to improve. Route support tickets and store reviews into the PMS process; name who reports incidents.

Sources. MDR

M-QMS-01: A quality management system covers the software life cycle

MUST · Level 1 (Legal baseline) · Users: all · Detection: manifest · Effort: high

Requirement. The manufacturer runs a quality management system (ISO 13485) with a software life cycle process (IEC 62304): requirements, architecture, verification, release, maintenance and problem resolution.

Why. MDR Art. 10(9) and Annex IX; harmonised standards give presumption of conformity.

How to improve. Set up the QMS before development of the device version; map the existing process to IEC 62304.

Sources. MDR, ISO-13485, IEC-62304

M-QUA-01: The class is assigned with reasons

MUST · Level 1 (Legal baseline) · Users: all · Detection: manifest · Effort: medium

Requirement. The software has a class under MDR Annex VIII (Rule 11 for most software) or IVDR Annex VIII, with the reasons written down, and the conformity route that follows from it.

Why. MDR Art. 51 and Annex VIII; MDCG 2019-11 rev. 1. Most software above class I needs a notified body.

How to improve. Fill qualification.class in hdsf.yaml and keep the reasoning in docs/qualification.md.

Sources. MDR, IVDR, MDCG-2019-11

M-RSK-01: Risk management covers clinical, use and security risks

MUST · Level 1 (Legal baseline) · Users: all · Detection: manual · Effort: high

Requirement. A risk management file (ISO 14971) covers wrong outputs, use errors, misuse, cybersecurity and data quality, with controls and residual risk accepted by a named person.

Why. MDR Annex I, Chapter I and section 17 (software and cybersecurity).

How to improve. Keep docs/risk-management.md under change control; review it with each release.

Sources. MDR, ISO-14971

M-USE-01: Usability engineering covers use errors

MUST · Level 2 (Forward-compliant) · Users: all · Detection: manual · Effort: high

Requirement. The manufacturer applies a usability engineering process: user profiles and use environments, use-related risk analysis, formative tests during design and a summative test with representative users before release.

Why. MDR Annex I (sections 5 and 22) asks to reduce risks from use errors; IEC 62366-1 is the harmonised standard for doing so.

How to improve. Write the usability engineering file; run a summative test with real users before each major release.

Sources. MDR, IEC-62366-1

M-WCH-01: The MDR and IVDR revision is tracked

SHOULD · Level 3 (Best practice) · Users: all · Detection: manual · Effort: low

Requirement. Someone follows the targeted MDR and IVDR revision and reports changes to classification and software rules to the team.

Why. The Commission proposal of 16 Dec 2025 changes software classification and other rules; it is with Parliament and Council.

How to improve. Add the procedure to a watch list; review at each quarterly planning.

Sources. MDR-REV)

R-CLN-01: Clinical investigations of devices follow the MDR

MUST · Level 1 (Legal baseline) · Users: patients (people under care or treatment), public (people managing their own health or wellbeing) · Detection: manual · Effort: high

Requirement. A study to gather clinical evidence for medical device software follows MDR Chapter VI: protocol, ethics committee opinion, competent authority notification or authorisation, informed consent, and safety reporting.

Why. MDR Art. 62 to 82.

How to improve. Plan the investigation with the competent authority's procedure (INFARMED in Portugal).

Sources. MDR
