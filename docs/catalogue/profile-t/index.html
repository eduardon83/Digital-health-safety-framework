E-DAT-01: Professionals see records only in a care relationship

MUST · Level 1 (Legal baseline) · Users: all · Detection: manual · Effort: medium

Requirement. Professionals can open a person's record only when they are part of that person's care, under professional secrecy, and only the parts they need.

Why. GDPR Art. 9(2)(h) and 9(3).

How to improve. Tie access rights to care episodes; review access rights monthly.

National note. Portugal: only health professionals may access the clinical record (Lei 12/2005, Art. 5); access on a need-to-know basis (Lei 58/2019, Art. 29).

Sources. GDPR, PT-LEI-12-2005, PT-LEI-58-2019

T-DAT-01: Remote consultations are private

MUST · Level 1 (Legal baseline) · Users: all · Features: telehealth · Detection: manual · Effort: medium

Requirement. Video, audio and chat consultations are encrypted in transit, ideally end to end, are not recorded without the patient's informed agreement, and do not run through consumer tools without a processing agreement.

Why. GDPR Art. 9(2)(h), 28 and 32; ISO 27799.

How to improve. Use a health-grade video provider under a DPA; turn recording off by default.

Sources. GDPR, ISO-27799

T-DAT-02: Remote care is recorded in the clinical record

MUST · Level 1 (Legal baseline) · Users: patients (people under care or treatment) · Features: telehealth · Detection: manual · Effort: medium

Requirement. The outcome of each remote consultation is recorded in the clinical record as national law requires, and the patient can access it.

Why. GDPR Art. 15. National law on clinical records applies.

How to improve. Integrate the consultation notes with the record system; give patients access.

National note. Portugal: health information, including clinical data, belongs to the person (Lei 12/2005, Art. 3).

Sources. GDPR, PT-LEI-12-2005

T-GOV-01: Professionals on the platform are verified

SHOULD · Level 3 (Best practice) · Users: all · Detection: manual · Effort: medium

Requirement. The platform checks the identity and licence of each professional before they see patients, and re-checks yearly.

Why. Health app assessment frameworks look at who gives care and with what qualification (ISO/TS 82304-2).

How to improve. Check against the national register (Ordem dos Médicos and others in Portugal); show the licence in the profile.

Sources. ISO-82304-2

T-SEC-01: Care providers meet NIS2

MUST · Level 1 (Legal baseline) · Users: all · Detection: manifest · Effort: high

Requirement. A care provider in scope of NIS2 (and its national law) registers with the national authority, applies the risk management measures, reports significant incidents and trains its management body.

Why. NIS2 Art. 20, 21 and 23; health is a sector of high criticality.

How to improve. Check scope and register; map measures to the national reference framework.

National note. Portugal: Decreto-Lei n.º 125/2025, in force 3 Apr 2026, with CNCS Regulamento n.º 756/2026; management bodies can be personally liable.

Sources. NIS2, PT-NIS2
