# Glossary

One term per thing. HDSF texts use these words with these meanings ([STYLE.md](../STYLE.md)).

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Art. 9 condition | One of the exceptions in GDPR Art. 9(2) that allow processing health data, for example explicit consent or health care under professional secrecy |
| BLOCK, REVIEW, WARN, HUMAN, WAIVED | The five kinds of finding in a report: fix before launch; needs a decision; recommended fix; a person must check; accepted with a reason |
| Controller, processor | GDPR roles: who decides why and how data is processed, and who processes it for them |
| CSEF | Child-Safe EdTech Framework, the sister framework for children's digital products; it shares HDSF's engine |
| DPIA | Data protection impact assessment (GDPR Art. 35); in Portuguese AIPD |
| EHDS | European Health Data Space, Regulation (EU) 2025/327 |
| EHR system | Electronic health record system, as defined in the EHDS |
| Engine (sfe) | The shared code that validates rules, scans products and writes reports for CSEF and HDSF |
| Feature | Something the product does that changes which rules apply (wearables, AI, chat, research use) |
| Finding | A sign, found in the code, the manifest or the documents, that a rule may not be met |
| GDPR (RGPD) | General Data Protection Regulation, Regulation (EU) 2016/679; in Portuguese Regulamento Geral sobre a Proteção de Dados |
| Health data | Data concerning health in the sense of GDPR Art. 4(15), including data that reveals health when linked to a person |
| Intended purpose | What the maker says the product is for, for whom and what it claims; it decides whether the product is a medical device |
| Level | How binding a rule is: 1 law, 2 EU recommendations and law that applies later, 3 best practice from research |
| Maker | The company or person that makes the product (manufacturer, in MDR terms) |
| Manifest | `hdsf.yaml`: one page that describes the product for HDSF |
| MDAI | Medical device artificial intelligence: AI that is, or is part of, a medical device (MDCG 2019-11 rev. 1) |
| MDSW | Medical device software |
| No-go | A rule a company pack marks as never broken; it blocks at every level |
| Pack | A file that adds sources and rules to HDSF or makes rules stricter: a country pack (Portugal), a sector pack or a company pack (ACME) |
| Profile | A group of rules for a kind of product: core, W wellness, M medical device software, E EHR systems and EHDS, R research, T telehealth |
| Qualification | The decision whether a product is a medical device (MDR or IVDR), and which class |
| Users | The people a product is for: the public, patients, professionals, children |
| Waiver | A recorded, dated decision to accept a finding for now, with a reason |
| Workbench | The browser page where people without code describe a product, add documents and get reports |
