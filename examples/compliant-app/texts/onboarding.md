# Onboarding texts (example)

Welcome to Calm Steps. This app helps you build small habits. It is not a medical device. It is not an emergency service: in an emergency, call 112.

You choose what to track. You can change this at any time. Your data stays in the EU.

Every week you see a short summary. You can share it with someone you trust. You decide what to share.

Walking a little more each day can help you feel better. Start with ten minutes. Add five minutes each week. Rest when you need to.
