# Privacy policy (example)

Example Wellbeing, Lda. is the data controller. You can contact us at privacy@example.org. Our data protection officer is reachable at dpo@example.org.

We use your data to give you weekly summaries of your steps, sleep and breathing sessions. These are health data, a special category of data. We only use them with your explicit consent, which you can withdraw at any time in Settings. Our legal basis for the account itself is the contract with you.

Our hosting provider in Ireland stores your data for us under a processing contract. We do not share your data with advertisers. We do not transfer your data outside the EU.

We keep your data until you delete your account. If you do not use the app for 24 months, we delete your account.

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, portability and the right to object. Write to us and we answer within one month. You can also lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority, the CNPD.

We do not make automated decisions about you.
