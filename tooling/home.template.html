Health Data Safety Framework

Open, checkable rules for digital health products in the EU: wellness apps, medical device software, health records, research platforms and telehealth. One stop shop for a three layer revision: regulation, recommendations and best practices and how your product is respecting them.

{rules} rules, {sources} sources. Each rule says what to do, why (with its source), how to improve and how it is checked: in the code, in a short manifest (hdsf.yaml) or by a person.

HDSF is a draft and not legal or regulatory advice. It is meant as a pre-check process to prevent rework. Whether software is a medical device is the manufacturer's decision under the MDR or IVDR; ask a regulatory expert or a notified body when in doubt.
