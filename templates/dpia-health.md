# DPIA outline for a digital health product: [product name]

Use with your data protection authority's DPIA template. HDSF rule CORE-DAT-03.

1. **Description.** Purposes, data (mark health, genetic, biometric and "reveals health" fields; CORE-DAT-01),
   people concerned (including children or patients), systems, processors and locations.
2. **Legal basis.** For each purpose: Art. 6 basis and Art. 9(2) condition (CORE-DAT-02). National rules that apply.
3. **Necessity and proportionality.** Minimisation (CORE-DAT-05), retention (CORE-DAT-06), information to people
   (CORE-COM-01), rights (CORE-DAT-09), processors (CORE-DAT-07), transfers (CORE-DAT-08).
4. **Risks to people.** Disclosure of a condition (lock screen, shared device, breach), discrimination (insurance,
   work), wrong health advice, loss of access to care data, tracking and profiling, re-identification of research data.
5. **Measures.** Encryption (CORE-DAT-12), no trackers on health screens (CORE-TRK-01), neutral notifications
   (CORE-COM-03), access control and logging (CORE-SEC-03, CORE-SEC-07), pseudonymisation (CORE-DAT-11), AI limits
   (CORE-AI-03, CORE-AI-04), incident plan (CORE-SEC-04).
6. **Residual risk and sign-off.** DPO opinion; prior consultation (Art. 36) if high risk remains.
7. **Review date.**
